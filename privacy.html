Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

NobleKey Stays provides furnished short-stay property marketing, inquiry and booking coordination services in Egypt. We also operate a private content-management workflow for reviewing property marketing videos before any authorized social-media publishing.

Information we process

Depending on your interaction with us, this may include your name, telephone number, WhatsApp messages, preferred location, stay dates, budget, booking inquiries, property-owner contact and listing information, and communications with our team. For our TikTok integration, when a TikTok account holder explicitly authorizes access, we may process the account identifiers, authorization tokens, selected permissions, video files, captions, publishing decisions, publishing status, and related technical logs needed to operate that integration.

How we use information

We use information to respond to guest and owner inquiries, coordinate property availability and booking requests, manage property listings, prepare and review marketing content, operate authorized social-media connections, maintain security, troubleshoot errors and comply with applicable legal requirements. Property availability and booking requests are subject to separate confirmation.

TikTok integration and permissions

Our TikTok integration is intended to let an authorized NobleKey Stays operator connect a TikTok account and manage approved property marketing content through TikTok's official developer interfaces. We request only the TikTok permissions needed for the enabled functions. Content is not automatically published solely because a video is created or reviewed; publishing requires explicit authorization and applicable platform and rights checks. TikTok processes information under its own policies when its services are used.

Sharing and service providers

We may share the information reasonably needed for an inquiry or booking with the relevant guest or property owner, and may use service providers and platform operators, including Meta/WhatsApp, TikTok, and infrastructure hosting or technical services, for the stated purposes. We do not sell personal information.

Storage, security and retention

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards and limit access according to operational need. Information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, operational records, dispute handling or legal requirements. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with you, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. You can revoke TikTok app access using your TikTok account settings. Revoking access stops future authorized API access but may not erase records that must be retained lawfully. To make a request, contact NobleKey Stays via the WhatsApp number below.

Changes

We may update this policy when services or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
