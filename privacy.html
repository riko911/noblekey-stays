Privacy Policy - NobleKey Stays

Last updated: October 5, 2026

NobleKey Stays provides short-stay accommodation inquiry and booking assistance. We may receive information you provide such as your name, phone number, WhatsApp messages, requested area, stay dates, guest requirements, budget, property preferences, and property details.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, match guests with suitable stays, manage booking requests, communicate with guests and property owners, provide support, prevent misuse, and maintain service records.

WhatsApp

When you contact us through WhatsApp, message and account information may be processed through Meta WhatsApp Business Platform and infrastructure used to operate our customer communication service.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information reasonably necessary to handle a booking or property inquiry with relevant guests, property owners, service providers, or when required by law.

Retention and security

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for operations, support, record keeping, dispute handling, and legal obligations, and use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information you provided, subject to records we must retain for legitimate business or legal reasons.
